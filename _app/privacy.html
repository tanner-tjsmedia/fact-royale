Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Fact Royale is a daily quiz. It needs very little about you, and this page says exactly what it keeps and why, in the order you would actually ask.

Who is responsible

Fact Royale is run by Takt16, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Takt16 is the data controller for everything described on this page. For anything here, write to us through the contact form in the footer of any page.

What we collect

If you only play

You do not need an account. The moment you open a quiz, we create an anonymous identifier for your browser. It is a random string. It is not linked to your name, your email, or anything else about you, and we use it for one thing: to record which answer you gave to which question, so the same question cannot be answered twice. Your answers and your score are stored against that identifier.

If you create an account

We ask for your first name, last name, a display name, and an email address. If you sign in with Google instead, we receive your name and email address from Google. We store these so that you can sign back in, so the leaderboard can show who you are, and so we can reach you about your account.

Alongside that we keep your quiz record: which days you played, how many questions you answered, how many you got right, your streak, and your results by category. That is the product — without it there is no streak and no Royal Record.

If you contact us or ask to be notified

Your message, your email address, and the topic you picked are sent to a Google Sheet we use as an inbox. If you asked to be told when the quiz returns, we keep your name and email on that sheet until we have told you.

Notifications

If — and only if — you accept the browser prompt, we store a push token so we can send you a daily reminder. Declining costs you nothing else. You can revoke it in your browser settings at any time.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. There is no advertising network on this site, no third-party analytics beyond what Firebase provides, and no tracking pixels.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the GDPR we rely on two grounds. Your account details and quiz record are held because they are necessary to provide the service you asked for — you cannot have a leaderboard position without a name on it. Notifications and the notify-me list rely on your consent, which you give by opting in and can withdraw at any time.

Who else sees it

Two companies process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Apps Script and Sheets) stores your account, your quiz record and any message you send us.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) serves the website itself and will see your IP address in its server logs, as any web host does.

Both are US companies and data may be processed outside the EU under the transfer mechanisms in their own terms.

How long we keep it

Your account and quiz record are kept for as long as the account exists. Delete the account and they go with it. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for as long as they are useful to answer, and no more than 24 months.

What you can ask for

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, hand it to you in a portable form, or stop using it. You can withdraw consent for notifications without affecting anything else. Ask through the contact form and we will answer within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to your national data protection authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, which is the authority for where Takt16 is established.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Firebase sets what it needs to keep you signed in, and the site keeps a few things in your browser's local storage — which quiz you are part-way through, whether you have dismissed a prompt. Clearing your browser data clears them, and the site still works.

Children

Fact Royale is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the site rather than quietly editing the date at the top.
