Kendrick Lamar's good kid, m.A.A.d city carries the subtitle “A Short Film”. Why?
AThe album was written as a screenplay first
BEach song was released with its own music video×
CThe tracks tell one night in Compton out of order✓
DIt was recorded live in one continuous session
Not quite. The answer was C.
The cover carries the subtitle “A Short Film by Kendrick Lamar”. It follows a teenage Kendrick through a single day and night in Compton, told deliberately out of order, with voicemails from his parents marking the timeline rather than the tracklist doing it.
Remember itOne day, out of order, voicemails for timestamps — documentary specific, not generic. That specificity is the thing critics kept pointing at, and it is why DAMN. later took a Pulitzer.
